How to Play — 30 second briefing

  • Goal: clear 5 waves of aliens. Shoot them; dodge or shoot down their red bullets.
  • Gravity: your ship sinks — heavier sinks faster. Hold thrust to stay up. Stay out of the glowing atmosphere at the bottom.
  • Guns fire at range; close-combat weapons launch by themselves every 2.5 seconds at the nearest alien — you can't aim them. In waves 1-3 one hit kills; from wave 4 it takes 2 hits.
  • Loot: dead aliens drop ● coins (points) — fly into them. Rarely: ⬡ shield, ★ bonus points.
  • Danger grows: damage you take gets tougher every wave. After every wave, hull below 90% is restored to 90%.
  • Hazards: asteroids, micrometeoroids, radiation, solar flares. Warnings appear if you identified them.
  • Team score is shared; each pilot's own score is tracked on the scoreboard.

Controls
