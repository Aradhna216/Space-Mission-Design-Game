How to Play — 30 second briefing

  • Goal: clear 5 waves of aliens. Shoot them; dodge or shoot down their red bullets.
  • Gravity: your ship sinks — heavier sinks faster. Hold thrust to stay up. Stay out of the glowing atmosphere at the bottom.
  • Guns fire at range; close-combat weapons launch by themselves every 2.5 seconds at the nearest alien — you can't aim them.
  • Loot: dead aliens drop coins; pick them up to score points.
  • Danger grows: damage you take gets tougher every wave.
  • Hazards: asteroids, radiation, and solar flare events can appear.

Controls

Move with A D or arrow keys. Fire with Space, thrust with W or ↑, and dive with S or ↓.
